The HB difference

A relationship,
not a subscription.

Creative requirements rarely arrive in convenient, predictable packages. Some weeks you need the familiarity and responsiveness of people who know your business inside out. At other times you need additional capacity, a particular specialist or an entire creative team to help deliver something extraordinary.

HB brings the right people and expertise together around your requirements, with flexible ways of working that adapt as your business changes.

No predetermined subscription packages or artificial limits on the kind of creative support you can access. We agree the right people, scope and commercial arrangement for your business, whether that means a dedicated team, support for a particular programme or access to specialist expertise when you need it.

You work directly with talented creative people who become part of your team, supported by an independent agency that can bring additional resources together whenever your requirements change.

Your people. Our people. One team.

How we work

Built around
your business

There is no single way to work with HB. We build the right team and operating model around your organisation, your existing people, your workload and your ambitions.

Whether you need a consistent creative presence within your business, additional capacity for a particular period or specialist expertise for a major project, we establish an arrangement that works for you.

Model 01

Your team, in-house.

Dedicated HB creatives working alongside your colleagues, immersed in your business, your culture and your brand.

Our people become part of your everyday marketing operation, building the understanding and relationships that make creative delivery more responsive, consistent and effective.

Model 02

Our studios, at your service.

Access the capabilities of our London and Cape Town studios, bringing additional creative capacity, specialist skills and production expertise into your organisation whenever they are required.

Our teams work with yours to support everything from everyday creative requirements to complex, large-scale programmes.

Model 03

The best of both.

Combine a dedicated in-house team with the resources of the wider HB agency.

Your embedded creatives provide continuity, brand knowledge and day-to-day support, while our studios bring additional people and expertise into the relationship as your requirements change.

The right people. The right expertise. The right arrangement.

Our work

Everything your marketing team needs to deliver.

From the everyday creative work that keeps your business moving to the ambitious projects that move it forward, HB brings together the skills, experience and creative judgement to support your marketing team wherever it needs us.

  1. Brand strategy, visual identity, brand guidelines, design systems, creative direction and the everyday application of your brand across your organisation.

  2. Campaign concepts, creative development, content, digital advertising, social media, email marketing and multichannel brand activation.

  3. Corporate presentations, pitch materials, sales enablement, reports, internal communications and essential business documents.

  4. Websites, digital experiences, digital design, motion graphics, animation, video and interactive content.

  5. Artwork, campaign adaptation, template development, versioning, localisation and the ongoing production of high-quality creative assets across channels and markets.

  6. Access to the wider HB agency for brand strategy, marketing thinking, creative direction, specialist digital projects and more ambitious creative programmes.

Whatever your organisation needs to communicate, create or deliver, we can bring the right combination of talent and expertise together to make it happen.
